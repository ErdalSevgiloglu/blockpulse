Block Pulse

Endless 8×8 block puzzle · Support

How to play

Drag the pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a full row or column to clear it and score points. Clear several lines at once or in a row for combos. The game ends when none of the remaining pieces fit.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, your best score, level and themes are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

How do I turn off sound or music? Open the settings (gear icon) on the home screen.

Why do I see ads? The game is free and supported by ads. You can watch an optional rewarded ad to continue a game.

How do I change ad tracking? iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
