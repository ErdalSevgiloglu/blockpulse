Privacy Policy

Block Pulse (iOS) · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Block Pulse is a block puzzle game developed by Erdal Sevgiloğlu ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

1. Data stored on your device

Your scores, best score, level, daily goals, unlocked themes and settings (sound, music, vibration) are stored only on your device. The game has no user accounts and we do not run any servers that receive this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interactions (views, clicks). This data is processed by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before your advertising identifier may be used to show personalised ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

4. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before personalised ads are used. You can accept or decline, and non-personalised ads may be shown if you decline.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and we do not sell personal data.

6. Children

Block Pulse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at My Ad Center.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: erdalsevgiloglu@gmail.com
